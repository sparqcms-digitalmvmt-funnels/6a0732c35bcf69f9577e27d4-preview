How Does the 3Doodle Pro Work?
Similar to glue guns, 3D pens melt plastic filament instead of glue sticks. The melted filament flows through the nozzle, allowing precise control over where it goes. Beyond art projects, these pens can create unique jewelry, custom items, and even fix household items like broken scissors. While kids will have a blast, adults will discover countless creative and practical uses too.The 3Doodle Pro features a stepless speed control, allowing you to adjust the flow of material seamlessly. Its ultrasonic sealed nozzle minimizes clogs, providing market-leading durability and reliability.
